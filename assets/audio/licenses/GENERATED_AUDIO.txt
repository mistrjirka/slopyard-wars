Slopyard Wars — original generated audio
=======================================

The 22 generated WAV files listed below were synthesized specifically for this project
using scripts/generate_audio.cjs. No audio samples, recordings, sound packs,
third-party compositions, synthesis libraries or downloaded music were used.

Source / authoring method: original project code; deterministic waveform,
noise, envelope, modulation and delay synthesis. The music is an original
16-bar downtempo arrangement (80 BPM) titled "Slopyard Afterhours".

Third-party sources and third-party audio licenses for these 22 files: none.
These are project-authored assets; this note does not assign a new license
to the project or change the owner's existing licensing terms.

EXCEPTION: hinge_open.wav and hinge_close.wav now use a recorded CC0 sound.
Their separate license and source records are hinge_open.LICENSE.txt and
hinge_close.LICENSE.txt. The source MP3 is also covered by those records.

The complete asset inventory, durations and signal levels are recorded in
assets/audio/manifest.json. Regenerate with:

    node scripts/generate_audio.cjs
